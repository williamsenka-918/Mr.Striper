What We Offer

Parking Lot Striping Services Built Around Your Property

From a brand new layout to a quick fire lane touch up, here's exactly what I can do for your lot, and what's included in each one.

Full Layout & Striping

New Striping Layouts

If your lot is brand new, recently repaved, or the current layout just doesn't work anymore, I'll start from scratch with you. I measure the space myself, plan a layout around how people actually park and move through your property, and lay down clean, straight lines you can count on.

  • Full remeasure of your lot before anything is planned
  • A layout planned for your traffic flow, not just the old lines
  • Straight, evenly spaced stalls that maximize usable parking
  • ADA-compliant accessible spaces included in the plan
Get a Quote for a New Layout

How long before I can reopen the lot?

Fresh lines are usually dry to the touch within 15 to 30 minutes and ready for light traffic in about 1 to 2 hours, so most lots reopen the same day.

Refresh & Restripe

Restriping

If your current layout still works and the lines just look tired, you don't need a whole new plan, you need a clean repaint. I'll walk your existing layout, touch up what's faded, and get your stalls looking sharp again without changing what already works for your property.

  • A full repaint over your existing, working layout
  • Faded lines, arrows, and stall markings refreshed
  • Straight, consistent lines that match your original spacing
  • A quick walkthrough first, so nothing gets missed
Get a Quote for a Restripe

Do I need a full layout or just a restripe?

If your current setup works for your traffic and parking count, a restripe is usually all you need. I'll tell you honestly if a full layout would serve you better instead.

Accessibility & Compliance

ADA Compliant Stalls & Signage

Accessible parking isn't optional, and it isn't something to guess at. I lay out van-accessible and standard accessible stalls to the correct dimensions and stall counts for your lot size, and install the signage that goes with them, so your property stays compliant and welcoming to everyone who visits.

  • Accessible stall counts based on your total parking spaces
  • Correct dimensions for standard and van-accessible stalls
  • Cross-hatched access aisles striped to spec
  • Required signage installed alongside the striping
Get a Quote for ADA Compliance

How many accessible stalls does my lot need?

It depends on your total stall count. I'll calculate the right number for your lot size and make sure the layout meets code before any paint goes down.

Fire Lanes & Curb Marking

Fire Lanes & Curb Painting

Blocked or unmarked fire lanes put people at risk and put the responsibility back on you as the property owner. I paint and stencil fire lanes clearly, so there's no question where emergency vehicles need access, and your property stays up to code.

  • Red curb painting along designated fire lanes
  • "NO PARKING FIRE LANE" stenciling
  • Clear, code-compliant lane marking
  • Touch-ups for existing fire lanes that have faded
Get a Quote for Fire Lane Marking

Will this match what our local fire department looks for?

I stripe and stencil fire lanes using the standard red curb and lettering most local fire departments require, and I'll flag anything on your lot that looks off so you can get ahead of it.

That's fine, most people aren't. Send me a few details about your lot and I'll tell you honestly what it actually needs.
