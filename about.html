About Mr. Striper

Built on Doing the Job Right the First Time

Mr. Striper is a Tulsa-based, owner-operated striping company. Here's the story behind it, and what you can expect when you work with us.

How It Started

Why I Started Mr. Striper

Parking lot striping is one of those jobs that's easy to overlook until it isn't. Faded lines don't just look bad — they cause real problems. Cars park crooked, accessible stalls stop reading as accessible, and fire lanes go unmarked. I started Mr. Striper because property owners in the Tulsa area deserve a straightforward way to fix that, without chasing down subcontractors or guessing at pricing.

I handle the work myself, from the first walkthrough to the final coat. That means the person quoting the job is the same person doing it, and the same person you can call afterward if something doesn't look right.

My goal is simple: show up when I say I will, quote it straight, and leave the lot looking like it should.
