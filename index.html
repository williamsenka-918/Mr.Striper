The part most lots get wrong

ADA compliance isn't optional

Most lots I walk are out of compliance and the owner has no idea. It's usually a cheap fix — until someone files a complaint.

$75,000

Where federal ADA fines start for a first violation, before legal costs.

  1. Not enough accessible stalls. A 100-stall lot needs four, with at least one van-accessible. Lots expand; the striping rarely catches up.
  2. No van-accessible space. One in every six accessible stalls has to be van-accessible. Older lots often have none.
  3. Access aisle too narrow. Five feet minimum, eight for van stalls, hatched so nobody parks in it.
  4. Missing signage. A painted symbol on the ground doesn't count. You need a sign at least 60 inches off the pavement.
